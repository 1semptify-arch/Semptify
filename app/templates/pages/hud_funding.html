HUD Funding Guide

Learn about HUD housing assistance programs.

7 programs available

Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8)

Rental assistance for low-income families, elderly, and disabled individuals to afford safe housing in the private market.

Eligibility: Low income | How to apply

Public Housing

Affordable rental housing for eligible low-income families, managed by local housing authorities.

Eligibility: Low income | How to apply

Continuum of Care (CoC)

Community-based programs designed to end homelessness through housing and support services.

Eligibility: Homeless individuals | How to apply

Emergency Solutions Grant (ESG)

Funds for emergency shelter, rapid re-housing, and homelessness prevention activities.

Eligibility: Homeless or at-risk | How to apply

Title I Property Improvement Loan

Federally insured loans for home repairs, improvements, and accessibility modifications.

Eligibility: Homeowners | How to apply

Section 203(k) Rehabilitation Mortgage

Financing for both purchase and rehabilitation of a home in a single mortgage.

Eligibility: Homebuyers | How to apply

Fair Housing Initiatives Program (FHIP)

Programs to prevent and address housing discrimination through education and enforcement.

Eligibility: All | How to apply
