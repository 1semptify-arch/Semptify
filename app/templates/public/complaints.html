Filing a Housing Complaint

Semptify's complaint wizard walks you through filing a housing complaint step by step. It covers common complaint types:

🏠

Habitability

Repairs not made, unsafe living conditions, lack of essential services.

⚖️

Discrimination

Treated differently based on race, religion, family status, disability, or other protected class.

🛡️

Retaliation

Landlord retaliated after you complained, requested repairs, or exercised your rights.

💰

Security Deposit

Deposit not returned, improper deductions, no itemization provided.

How It Works

  1. Describe your situation — The wizard asks plain-language questions.
  2. Gather your documents and records — Attach documents, photos, and communications from your vault.
  3. Identify the right agency — The wizard routes your complaint to the correct authority.
  4. Draft and file — Generate a complaint document and file it.

We're Not Lawyers

Semptify is an organizational tool and educational resource — not a law firm. The complaint wizard helps you organize and draft, but it's not legal advice. For legal advice, contact a licensed attorney or your local legal aid society.
