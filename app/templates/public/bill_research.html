    How a figure earns its place. Every number on this site comes from a source you can read yourself. Before it publishes, it goes through three checks: (1) the source exists and says what we claim, (2) the math is re-derived independently, and (3) the figure is presented without spin. Fail any check and the figure waits — it does not publish.

    What you won't find. No people's names, no business names — private parties appear only as roles ("a metro provider," "a renter"), unless that party has given written consent to be named. Public agencies and published research sources are cited by name — that's how you check our work.
