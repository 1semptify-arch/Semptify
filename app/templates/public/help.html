Emergency Resources

If you're facing an immediate housing emergency (illegal lockout, unsafe conditions, or eviction within 24 hours), contact your local legal aid society or emergency services immediately.

Common Questions

  • Is Semptify free? Yes. Free for tenants, forever. No advertising, ever.
  • Where are my documents stored? In your own cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive). We never store them on our servers.
  • Can Semptify give me legal advice? No. Semptify is an organizational tool, not a law firm. For legal advice, contact a licensed attorney.
  • How do I get started? Click "Get Started" on the home page. We'll route you to the right place.
