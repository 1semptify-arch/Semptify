The Crisis

The Housing Crisis
Is a Legal Crisis

Millions of American renters face housing courts, illegal evictions, and habitability violations every year — alone, uninformed, and without records. The system was never built for them.

3.6M
Eviction Filings Per Year
The United States sees approximately 3.6 million eviction filings annually — one every nine seconds. Most never go to trial. Most tenants have no idea what their rights are.
9 in 10
Landlords Have Legal Representation
In housing court, the power imbalance is stark. Nearly 90% of landlords show up with an attorney. They know the process. They know the paperwork. They know how to win.
1 in 10
Tenants Have Legal Help
Fewer than 10% of tenants in housing proceedings have access to a lawyer. The rest face legal proceedings — often with eviction or homelessness on the line — completely alone.
"I didn't know I had rights. By the time I found out, it was too late." — The experience of millions of renters who face housing court without knowledge, records, or representation

Housing Cost Burden

The average American now spends 30–50% or more of their income on housing. For low-income renters, that number routinely exceeds 60–70%, leaving nothing for emergencies, legal help, or advocacy.

Habitability Violations

Millions of renters live in units with mold, broken heat, pest infestations, or structural hazards — and don't know how to document violations, file complaints, or legally withhold rent without risking eviction.

Communities Most Affected

The burden falls disproportionately on Black, Brown, immigrant, elderly, and disabled renters — communities already navigating systemic disadvantage. Eviction is not a housing event; it's a life event with cascading consequences.

The Solution

We Built the Tools That
Should Have Always Existed

Semptify is non-adversarial. We don't fight landlords — we give tenants the knowledge, records, and community they need to stand with composure and clarity. Six functions. One platform. Tenants never pay.

01 — INFORMATION
Know Your Rights Before the Dispute Starts

Plain-language, state-by-state explanations of tenant rights — notice requirements, habitability standards, illegal fees, retaliation protections, and more. Accessible to everyone, not just lawyers.

02 — RESEARCH
Go Deeper on Your Specific Situation

Not every situation is the same. Semptify's research layer helps tenants explore the statutes, precedents, and agency rules relevant to their exact case — without needing to know where to look.

03 — DOCUMENTATION
A Paper Trail You Can Trust

Every communication, payment, repair request, and notice — documented, timestamped, and organized. A complete paper trail stored in your own cloud, ready if you ever need it in court or with a mediator.

04 — ORGANIZE
Scattered Texts, Photos, Receipts → Coherent Record

Most tenants have evidence — it's just scattered across phone photos, email threads, text messages, and handwritten notes. Semptify organizes everything into a coherent, chronological record that tells your story clearly.

05 — COLLABORATE
Find Your Tenants Union. Build One If It Doesn't Exist.

Tenants are stronger together. Semptify connects renters with their local tenants unions, tenant advocacy organizations, and community support networks — and provides tools for organizing where nothing yet exists.

06 — BE HEARD
File Complaints. Reach Legislators. Tell Your Story.

Semptify's Public Exposure Suite connects tenants to journalists, housing advocates, and elected officials. The Complaint Wizard guides users through HUD, state AG, and code enforcement filings — step by step, plain language, no lawyer required.

Platform Features

17+ Modules,
Built to Matter

Every feature in Semptify answers a specific, real gap in the tenant experience. From the first lease signature to the last day in court.

Group A — Core Tenant Tools
Know Your Rights Law Library

State-by-state housing law in plain English. Covers notice requirements, security deposit rules, habitability standards, retaliation protections, and right-to-cure timelines. No law degree required.

Document Vault

Your tenant document vault lives in your own Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive account — not on Semptify's servers. Lease agreements, repair photos, payment receipts, notices: all organized and owned by you.

Unified Timeline

Every event in your tenancy — payments, notices, calls, repairs, inspections, disputes — presented as a single chronological timeline. The clearest possible picture of what happened, and when.

Case Builder

Automated documentation from your records. Case Builder takes your vault contents and timeline, identifies the strongest evidence, and assembles them into a clear, coherent case narrative ready for legal or advocacy use.

Complaint Wizard

Guided, step-by-step filing with HUD, state attorneys general, local housing authorities, and code enforcement agencies. The Wizard pre-fills forms from your vault data and walks you through every field in plain language.

Group B — Advanced & Legal Tools
Eviction Defense Module

Step-by-step guidance through the eviction process — from notice receipt to court date. Includes state-specific court procedure guides, timeline calculators, deadline tracking, and response templates drafted in plain language.

Court Forms System

Pre-filled legal documents drawn from your vault and timeline. Generates answers to eviction summons, motions for continuance, habitability defenses, and more — with state-specific formatting and instructions for filing pro se.

Litigation Intelligence

Pattern detection across public housing court records. Identifies serial evictors, landlords with histories of retaliation or code violations, and systemic practices — giving tenants and advocates actionable intelligence to level the playing field.

Fraud Exposure

Document and surface predatory landlord patterns: illegal fees, false security deposit claims, retaliatory lease terminations, habitability cover-ups, and discriminatory practices. Creates a documented record for regulatory and legal action.

Public Exposure Suite

Bridges the gap between individual tenants and systemic change. Connects tenants with housing journalists, policy advocates, and legislators. Makes individual stories part of a larger record — with privacy controls tenants control.

Forensic Evidence System — FEMS

Advanced evidence processing: OCR of scanned documents and photos, automatic deduplication, SMS and chat import, metadata preservation, and chain-of-custody logging. Turns a phone's camera roll into legally credible evidence.

Semptify Go — Mobile PWA In Development

Full Semptify functionality in a Progressive Web App — now in active development. Document in the moment — photograph a notice, import texts, check your rights, or access your vault — anywhere, on any device. Offline-capable, installable, fast.

Architecture

Your Data Never
Touches Our Servers

Semptify's storage-based authentication model is a genuine innovation in tenant-facing technology. Your cloud storage account is your identity. Your documents never leave your control — and our standing promise: nothing on our servers beyond what is absolutely necessary and non-identifiable. If that ever fails in production, the project doesn't deserve to run.

🔑

Zero Passwords

You connect Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive. There is no Semptify password to create, store, remember, or lose. Your own cloud storage confirms it's you.

📁

You Own Your Data

Every document, timeline entry, and case record is written back to your own cloud account — timeline data included, held in your own storage. Semptify holds zero personal files. If Semptify disappeared tomorrow, your records would still be exactly where you left them.

🌐

Portability

Your identity and your records travel with your cloud account. Switch devices, reinstall the app, or share access with a lawyer — your entire tenancy record comes with you, without re-registration.

Founded from Personal Experience

Semptify was created by Bradley Crowe following a personal navigation of a housing dispute. The experience made clear that the gap between tenants and their rights was not a knowledge problem — it was an access and systems problem. Semptify exists to close that gap, for everyone who faces it after him.
