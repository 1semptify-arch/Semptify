The Crisis

The Housing Crisis
Is a Legal Crisis

Millions of American renters face housing courts, illegal evictions, and habitability violations every year — alone, uninformed, and without records. The system was never built for them.

3.6M
Eviction Filings Per Year
The United States sees approximately 3.6 million eviction filings annually — one every nine seconds. Most never go to trial. Most tenants have no idea what their rights are.
9 in 10
Landlords Have Legal Representation
In housing court, the power imbalance is stark. Nearly 90% of landlords show up with an attorney. They know the process. They know the paperwork. They know how to win.
1 in 10
Tenants Have Legal Help
Fewer than 10% of tenants in housing proceedings have access to a lawyer. The rest face legal proceedings — often with eviction or homelessness on the line — completely alone.
"I didn't know I had rights. By the time I found out, it was too late." — The experience of millions of renters who face housing court without knowledge, records, or representation

Housing Cost Burden

The average American now spends 30–50% or more of their income on housing. For low-income renters, that number routinely exceeds 60–70%, leaving nothing for emergencies, legal help, or advocacy.

Habitability Violations

Millions of renters live in units with mold, broken heat, pest infestations, or structural hazards — and don't know how to document violations, file complaints, or legally withhold rent without risking eviction.

Communities Most Affected

The burden falls disproportionately on Black, Brown, immigrant, elderly, and disabled renters — communities already navigating systemic disadvantage. Eviction is not a housing event; it's a life event with cascading consequences.

The Solution

We Built the Tools That
Should Have Always Existed

Semptify is non-adversarial. We don't fight landlords — we give tenants the knowledge, records, and community they need to stand with composure and clarity. Six functions. One platform. Free forever.

01 — INFORMATION
Know Your Rights Before the Dispute Starts

Plain-language, state-by-state explanations of tenant rights — notice requirements, habitability standards, illegal fees, retaliation protections, and more. Accessible to everyone, not just lawyers.

02 — RESEARCH
Go Deeper on Your Specific Situation

Not every situation is the same. Semptify's research layer helps tenants explore the statutes, precedents, and agency rules relevant to their exact case — without needing to know where to look.

03 — DOCUMENTATION
A Paper Trail You Can Trust

Every communication, payment, repair request, and notice — documented, timestamped, and organized. A complete paper trail stored in your own cloud, ready if you ever need it in court or with a mediator.

04 — ORGANIZE
Scattered Texts, Photos, Receipts → Coherent Record

Most tenants have evidence — it's just scattered across phone photos, email threads, text messages, and handwritten notes. Semptify organizes everything into a coherent, chronological record that tells your story clearly.

05 — COLLABORATE
Find Your Tenants Union. Build One If It Doesn't Exist.

Tenants are more powerful together. Semptify connects renters with their local tenants unions, tenant advocacy organizations, and community support networks — and provides tools for organizing where nothing yet exists.

06 — BE HEARD
File Complaints. Reach Legislators. Tell Your Story.

Semptify's Public Exposure Suite connects tenants to journalists, housing advocates, and elected officials. The Complaint Wizard guides users through HUD, state AG, and code enforcement filings — step by step, plain language, no lawyer required.

Platform Features

17+ Modules,
Built to Matter

Every feature in Semptify answers a specific, real gap in the tenant experience. From the first lease signature to the last day in court.

Group A — Core Tenant Tools
Know Your Rights Law Library

State-by-state housing law in plain English. Covers notice requirements, security deposit rules, habitability standards, retaliation protections, and right-to-cure timelines. No law degree required.

Document Vault

Your tenant document vault lives in your own Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive account — not on Semptify's servers. Lease agreements, repair photos, payment receipts, notices: all organized and owned by you.

Unified Timeline

Every event in your tenancy — payments, notices, calls, repairs, inspections, disputes — presented as a single chronological timeline. The clearest possible picture of what happened, and when.

Case Builder

Automated documentation from your records. Case Builder takes your vault contents and timeline, identifies the strongest evidence, and assembles them into a clear, coherent case narrative ready for legal or advocacy use.

Complaint Wizard

Guided, step-by-step filing with HUD, state attorneys general, local housing authorities, and code enforcement agencies. The Wizard pre-fills forms from your vault data and walks you through every field in plain language.

AI Copilot

Powered by Azure AI and local Ollama models. The Copilot answers questions about your rights, analyzes your lease for problematic clauses, suggests documentation steps, and helps draft complaint letters — with full privacy: your documents stay in your cloud.

Group B — Advanced & Legal Tools
Eviction Defense Module

Step-by-step guidance through the eviction process — from notice receipt to court date. Includes state-specific court procedure guides, timeline calculators, deadline tracking, and response templates drafted in plain language.

Court Forms System

Pre-filled legal documents drawn from your vault and timeline. Generates answers to eviction summons, motions for continuance, habitability defenses, and more — with state-specific formatting and instructions for filing pro se.

Litigation Intelligence

Pattern detection across public housing court records. Identifies serial evictors, landlords with histories of retaliation or code violations, and systemic practices — giving tenants and advocates actionable intelligence to level the playing field.

Fraud Exposure

Document and surface predatory landlord patterns: illegal fees, false security deposit claims, retaliatory lease terminations, habitability cover-ups, and discriminatory practices. Creates a documented record for regulatory and legal action.

Public Exposure Suite

Bridges the gap between individual tenants and systemic change. Connects tenants with housing journalists, policy advocates, and legislators. Makes individual stories part of a larger record — with privacy controls tenants control.

Forensic Evidence System — FEMS

Advanced evidence processing: OCR of scanned documents and photos, automatic deduplication, SMS and chat import, metadata preservation, and chain-of-custody logging. Turns a phone's camera roll into legally credible evidence.

Semptify Go — Mobile PWA In Development

Full Semptify functionality in a Progressive Web App — now in active development. Document in the moment — photograph a notice, import texts, check your rights, or access your vault — anywhere, on any device. Offline-capable, installable, fast.

The Problem We Are Solving

The United States sees approximately 3.6 million eviction filings every year. Behind each filing is a renter who, in the vast majority of cases, received a notice they didn't fully understand, had no organized record of their tenancy, and had no accessible pathway to the legal knowledge that might have changed the outcome. Studies consistently show that when tenants receive any form of legal assistance or guidance, eviction rates drop significantly — but fewer than 10% of tenants in housing proceedings have access to even basic legal help.

The power imbalance is stark and structurally embedded: approximately 90% of landlords in housing court are represented by attorneys, while the overwhelming majority of tenants appear alone — without documentation, without knowledge of their procedural rights, and without the clarity to advocate for themselves. This is not a matter of personal failing; it is a systemic gap in access to legal information and organized support. Semptify was built specifically to close that gap.

Beyond eviction, millions of renters live with mold, broken heating systems, pest infestations, and other habitability violations they don't know how to address legally. Millions more pay illegal fees, lose security deposits unlawfully, or face retaliation for asserting rights they didn't know they had. The core problem is not that tenant rights don't exist — it is that tenants cannot access, understand, or act on those rights without expensive professional help that most simply cannot afford.

Our Use of Claude

Semptify's AI Copilot, powered by Claude, would serve tenants in four specific, high-impact ways. First, lease document analysis: tenants would be able to ask natural-language questions about their lease — "Is this late fee legal in my state?" "Does this clause allow my landlord to enter without notice?" — and receive plain-language answers grounded in their specific lease language and their state's housing statutes. This alone would represent a transformative democratization of legal literacy for millions of renters.

Second, plain-language rights explanation: tenants in all 50 states and D.C. would be able to ask questions about their specific rights — notice requirements, security deposit timelines, habitability standards, retaliation protections — and receive clear, accurate, jurisdiction-specific guidance rather than generic information that may not apply to their situation.

Third, documentation guidance: when a tenant is building a case — for a complaint, for court, or simply for their own records — Claude would help them understand what documentation is most important, how to describe events accurately, and how to organize their evidence into a coherent narrative. This guidance is the difference between a tenant walking into housing court with a pile of unsorted photos and one who arrives with a clear, timestamped record.

Fourth, complaint and correspondence drafting: the Complaint Wizard guides tenants through HUD, state AG, and code enforcement filings. Claude would assist in drafting the narrative sections of these complaints — helping tenants describe their situation clearly, accurately, and in the language that regulatory bodies respond to. This dramatically lowers the barrier to formal complaint filing, which is currently one of the most intimidating steps for tenants.

On privacy: Semptify's architecture means that tenant documents never leave their own cloud storage. When a tenant asks a question that references their lease, our system passes only the anonymized, relevant text to Claude — never the full document, never any personally identifiable information. All queries are processed with strict rate-limit compliance and response caching to ensure responsible API use.

Technical Implementation

Semptify is built on FastAPI with Python 3.11.9, an async architecture that integrates cleanly with Anthropic's API. Our implementation plan includes: async Claude API integration with full streaming support, response caching for common rights questions to minimize API calls, jurisdiction-aware prompt engineering for state-specific accuracy, rate-limit compliance with graceful degradation to local Ollama models when API limits are reached, and a full privacy layer that ensures no tenant PII is passed in Claude requests. We are also committed to ongoing prompt red-teaming to ensure the AI Copilot never provides advice that could harm a tenant's legal position.

Expected Outcomes

With Claude for Nonprofits access, we project the AI Copilot will serve tens of thousands of tenants in the first year of full operation, with meaningful concentration among users in eviction proceedings and active habitability disputes — the situations where timely, accurate guidance has the highest impact. We will track and report: number of lease analyses completed, rights questions answered, complaints successfully filed with AI assistance, and user-reported outcomes (resolved disputes, successful court hearings, avoided evictions).

Our broader goal is measurable: we want to shift the information asymmetry in housing courts, one tenancy at a time. Claude is the most capable model available for the nuanced, context-sensitive work of legal information communication — which is precisely why it is the model of choice for Semptify's most critical feature.

The Societal Challenge: Legal Access in Housing

The United States processes approximately 3.6 million eviction filings every year — a figure that represents millions of families, individuals, and households facing the potential loss of their home. The legal system governing evictions is structured such that approximately 90% of landlords in housing court are represented by attorneys, while fewer than 10% of tenants have any form of legal assistance. This is not an accident; it is the predictable outcome of a system that has never prioritized tenant access to legal knowledge.

The consequences of this gap extend far beyond housing. Eviction creates cascading harms: loss of employment due to address instability, disruption of children's schooling, damage to credit that makes future housing harder to secure, and severe mental health consequences for individuals and families. Research consistently shows that the outcome of housing proceedings is heavily determined by whether the tenant received any form of legal or informational assistance — and yet the infrastructure to provide that assistance at scale has never existed.

Generative AI represents the first genuinely scalable solution to this problem. The ability to provide accurate, jurisdictionally specific legal information in real time, to help tenants organize and understand their documentation, and to guide them through the procedural steps of complaint filing and court preparation — these are tasks that generative AI can now perform at a quality and cost point that makes universal access genuinely achievable for the first time. Semptify is the platform built to deploy this capability where it is most needed.

Semptify's Generative AI Implementation

Semptify's AI Copilot is the platform's core GenAI feature, and it is specifically designed for the complexity and stakes of tenant-facing legal information. The Copilot uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) against Semptify's Know Your Rights Law Library — covering all 50 states and DC — to provide jurisdictionally accurate, contextually relevant answers to tenant questions about their rights, their lease, and their legal options. This is not generic AI chat; it is a purpose-built legal information system that grounds every response in actual housing law.

With AWS Bedrock integration — funded by the Pathfinder award — Semptify would expand the AI Copilot to include: Claude 3.5 Sonnet via Bedrock for complex lease analysis and legal question answering; Amazon Titan for embeddings powering the law library search and similarity matching; AWS Comprehend for entity recognition in tenant documents (landlord names, dates, property addresses) to accelerate case building; and Amazon Textract for FEMS document OCR, replacing a currently manual-intensive process with accurate, scalable automated document processing.

The privacy architecture is critical: Semptify's storage-based authentication model means that tenant documents live in users' own Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive accounts. When documents are processed through AWS Bedrock-powered features, only the processed output — not the source documents — is retained, and only temporarily. This gives tenants the full benefit of enterprise AI capabilities without the data sovereignty concerns that are otherwise a significant barrier to adoption for vulnerable populations.
