Why stories matter here. Laws get written from summaries. The housing bill being drafted on this site gets written from what actually happened to people — the repair that took nine months, the deposit that never came back, the notice taped to the door.

How it works. You write it. We read every one. Before anything publishes, a review pass removes names, addresses, case numbers, and anything else that could identify you. What remains is the story itself — posted here and used to inform the bill and public policy.

The one rule. Please don't include your name, your landlord's name, property addresses, or court case numbers anywhere in what you write. Keep those out and the review stays quick.
