Our Mission

Semptify is a tenant rights organization building technology to protect and advance lawful tenant rights through documentation, education, and evidence preservation.

We are on the side of tenants — always. We advocate for tenants exercising their legal rights, not tenants breaking the law. Our tools help renters document their tenancy, understand their rights, and protect themselves when something goes wrong.

What We Believe

"Everyone deserves a safe, clean place to live.
Knowledge and documentation go further than confrontation."

We believe that most problems between tenants and landlords can be avoided with knowledge and preparation. By understanding what's expected of you — and what you can expect from your landlord — you can navigate your tenancy with confidence.

What We Do

Semptify builds free tools for tenants:

  • Document Organizer — Log rent payments, maintenance requests, and communications. Build a timeline of your tenancy.
  • Know Your Rights Library — Plain-language guides to tenant law in your state. Facts only, no opinions.
  • Document Storage — Keep your lease, photos, and receipts organized in your own cloud storage. We never store your documents.
  • Preparedness Tools — Template letters, deadline trackers, and educational resources about court procedures.

What We Are Not

Semptify is an organizational tool and educational resource — not a law firm. We can't give legal advice. For legal advice, contact a licensed attorney or your local legal aid society.

Our Commitments

  • Free for tenants, forever. No advertising, ever.
  • Privacy-respecting by design. Your documents stay in your control.
  • User-controlled storage. We never store your documents on our servers.
  • Evidence preservation over feature novelty. We build what protects tenants, not what's trendy.
  • Calm, clear, trustworthy UX. Simple enough for a stressed tenant to use immediately.
