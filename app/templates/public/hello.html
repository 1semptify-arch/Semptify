In the works: a housing bill proposal

We are working to put together a proposal for a bill for the housing industry, centered on clear, fair documentation standards. It is early. Nothing is filed. What follows is our early read — opinion, labeled as opinion, not settled fact — on how it might affect different people:

  • For renters: a common documentation standard could make it simpler to keep a clear, dated record of a tenancy — and simpler to show the facts when something goes wrong.
  • For housing providers: clear standards could reduce disputes, make expectations predictable, and make it easier for good operators to show they are good operators.
  • For lawmakers: better records across the industry could give policymakers real data about what is actually happening in housing, instead of competing anecdotes.

We will publish the draft and invite comment from every side as the work matures.
