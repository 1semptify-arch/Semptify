Partner With Semptify

Housing advocacy groups can partner with Semptify to refer tenants, share resources, and coordinate support. Semptify gives the tenants you help a document organizer they can use themselves — keeping records organized and accessible.

A tenant's records live in their own cloud storage, not ours. If they choose to share documents with you, they share them directly — nothing sits on our servers.

What your clients can use

Everything on Semptify is built for the tenant — the person living the situation. The tools they can use today:

  • A document organizer in their own cloud storage
  • A timeline of their tenancy — notices, repairs, conversations
  • The law library — verified statutes and court rules with sources
  • A guided complaint wizard — habitability, discrimination, retaliation, deposits
