Partner With Semptify

Housing advocacy groups can partner with Semptify to refer tenants, share resources, and coordinate support. Semptify gives your clients a document organizer they can use themselves — keeping records organized and accessible.

Tools for advocates — managing clients, reviewing documents, coordinating support — are planned as a separate add-on, not live today. To talk about a partnership, write to the partnerships address on the contact page.
