Why this page exists. The housing bill on this site only becomes law if legislators carry it — and legislators carry what the people in their districts ask for. One clear message from a constituent counts more than a hundred from anywhere else.

What this page is not. We do not contact anyone for you, we do not collect your information, and we do not run a petition. These are the government's own lookup tools and directories — you use them directly.

The bill is a Minnesota bill — these are the channels that can act on it.

What to say

No script needed — short and honest works best. A few things that help staff route your message:

  • Say you live in their district — that is what makes it count.
  • One or two sentences on what happened to you, or why renting needs the rule changed.
  • What you would like them to do — for example, support the renter-documentation bill being drafted in public on this site.
  • Your name and address go in their contact form, not in the message itself — that is how they verify you are a constituent.
