MINNESOTA RENT ASSURANCE & DEPOSIT PROTECTION ACT

Preamble / findings (drafting placeholder)

  • Deposit disputes and serial nonpayment filings consume a third to a

half of Minnesota's ~23–26K annual eviction filings.

  • Private "deposit insurance" products (Jetty, Rhino, Obligo) pay the

landlord and recover from the tenant — creating new debt, not

protection (Jetty class action, S.D.N.Y., filed Oct 6 2026).

  • Custodial deposit protection with free independent adjudication has

operated nationally in the UK since 2007, funded off pooled float.

Definitions (drafting placeholder)

  • "Residential landlord" — attaches to the rental relationship, not a

municipal license (MN has no statewide rental license).

  • "Pool," "Fund," "Administrator," "Adjudicator," "Covered hardship,"

"Manufactured claim," "Clean year."

TITLE I — DEPOSIT PROTECTION

*The proven, self-funding core. Amends Minn. Stat. §504B.178. Precedent:

UK Tenancy Deposit Protection (custodial schemes).*

**I-1. Custodial escrow.** Every residential security deposit is paid

into bonded program escrow at lease signing and held there **for the

tenancy**. The landlord never holds, itemizes, or returns the deposit.

Statutory 1% interest continues to accrue to the tenant.

*(Locked: DECISIONS 10/23 — UK custodial shape, supersedes clean-year

refund.)*

**I-2. Independent claims adjudication.** Deposit and damage claims are

decided by an independent adjudication body on an administrative-law

model. Neither the landlord nor the administering carrier decides

claims. *(Locked clause 3.)*

**I-3. Tenant counter-claim lane.** Tenants bring deposit claims through

the same body; the administrator answers to claimants, not landlords.

*(Locked clause 3.)*

**I-4. No subrogation.** A paid claim is not recoverable from the tenant

by any party. *(Locked clause 1 — applies across all titles.)*

**I-5. Claims-free dividend (renter).** Clean years earn the renter an

annual dividend declared from pool surplus — mutual model, not a

guaranteed payment. *(Locked 10/23.)*

**I-6. Public claims registry.** Aggregate per-owner claims data

published; accuracy and dispute rules modeled on FCRA. *(Locked clause 6;

Tier-3 privacy note.)*

**I-7. Manufactured-claim penalties.** A claim proven fabricated carries

a surcharge plus referral. Symmetric penalty for knowingly-false fraud

referrals against tenants; tenant notice and defense window before any

referral transmits. *(Locked clause 5 + Brad 10/19 referral-abuse

controls.)*

**I-8. Funding of Title I.** Pooled float above the statutory 1%

(~3% net of escrowed deposits, ~$23M/yr at scale) funds escrow

administration and adjudication. *(10/23 math audit.)* Adjudication

carries its own budget line in the actuarial ask, not buried in the

admin load.

**I-9. Deposit-alternative products.** Licensed security-deposit

replacement products may be offered in Minnesota only if they are

no-subrogation, refundable-principal, and report claims through the

registry. *(REMEDIATION H10 — convert the SDR industry, don't just ban

it. NEEDS BRAD: new clause, not yet in DECISIONS.)*

TITLE II — RENT ASSURANCE

*The guarantee-fund leg. Framed as a state guarantee fund administered

like insurance (UI precedent), not as a conventional insured peril.

Recommended path: MHFA pilot on LIHTC stock first; statewide rollout

contingent on pilot actuarials and the year-3 report.*

**II-1. Covered hardship.** The fund pays covered rent to the landlord

during verified tenant hardship from a defined trigger list (job loss,

medical crisis, documented per UI-style verification) for a defined

coverage term. *(Open drafting Q: trigger list + 2–6 month term — needs

actuarial.)*

**II-2. No subrogation; forward-rent plan only.** A paid claim is not

recoverable from the tenant. A repayment plan applies solely to rent

accruing after the coverage period and is a condition of continued

coverage, not of the claim. *(REMEDIATION H5 one-liner.)*

**II-3. Mandatory participation; three-source funding braid.**

  • Landlord matched share — community-rated base + bounded experience

surcharge (±band); clean-book years earn a **no-claim dividend**

from surplus. *(Locked 10/23.)*

  • Renter-side share carried by the MN Renter's Credit (M1PR) as a

**monthly advance** reconciled on the return (federal APTC/Form 8962

model); enrollment via the CRP e-file data rail; over-cap renters pay

the share directly (~$6/mo), disclosed. *(Locked 10/23; H2/H3/H8.)*

  • Deposit float (Title I) as the ongoing third leg.
  • Split ratio: open pending actuarial (~$3.25 + ~$6 + ~$6 ≈ $15/mo at

$1,300 rent, 10/23 restatement).

**II-4. Year-one ramp and backstop.** Coverage phases in as escrow

crosses a threshold; or a repayable state advance against future

premiums (UI trust-fund precedent), with legislative appropriation

backup. *(REMEDIATION H7 / Tier 3 — pick one; state it.)*

**II-5. Tenant-side controls.** Verified triggers, per-lease coverage

caps, claims-history pricing at renewal, repayment-plan cooperation.

*(Fairness axiom, DECISIONS 10/19.)*

**II-6. Pilot authority.** MHFA may operate Title II as a pilot on

publicly-subsidized (LIHTC) properties without further appropriation;

pilot data constitutes the fiscal note for statewide rollout. *(Concept

sheet "first session" option, made statutory.)*

TITLE IV — GOVERNANCE, HOME AGENCY, REVIEW

**IV-1. Governing board** with tenant and consumer seats (anti-capture).

*(Locked clause 4.)*

**IV-2. Home agency.** Fund at MHFA; adjudication at an independent body;

licensed-insurer administration contracted out (SFM precedent).

*(REMEDIATION H9 — answers Commerce-vs-MHFA. NEEDS BRAD confirm.)*

**IV-3. Review.** Administrative appeal → district court; standard of

review OPEN. *(Open drafting Q.)*

**IV-4. Sunset and report.** Five-year sunset with a mandatory year-3

report to the housing committees. *(Tier-3 recommendation: 5-yr, not

3-yr hard sunset — NEEDS BRAD; locked clause 7 says "sunset + year-3

report" without a term.)*

**IV-5. Single-subject conforming language.** Credit-advance mechanics

drafted as machinery for the fund, inside this Act, not as separate tax

policy. *(H12.)*

1. No subrogation — a paid claim ends the matter

2. Three-source funding braid — deposit float + M1PR advance + landlord

matched share; mandatory participation stands; rating = community

5. Manufactured-claim penalties

6. Public claims registry

7. Sunset + year-3 report

8. Renter's belongings coverage — third pool leg

(cap/deductible/opt-in-vs-mandatory = open drafting Qs)

Open drafting questions (for staff / Revisor / reviewers)

  • Home agency: MHFA or Commerce? (pilot runs at MHFA regardless) —

IV-2 proposes an answer; not locked.

  • Hardship trigger list + coverage term (2–6 month range, needs actuarial)
  • Interaction with FHPAP emergency assistance — supplement, don't duplicate
  • Premium rate cap vs. market-set with oversight?
  • Split ratio between credit advance and landlord match — rechecked

10/23: base ≈ net float ~$3.25/mo + ~$6 renter-credit + ~$6 landlord

match ≈ $15.33/mo at $1,300 rent. Net float = 3% of escrowed deposits

(4% yield less the 1% statutory interest paid to the tenant — the

earlier $4.33 figure counted the tenant's own 1%). Float is an

ONGOING leg (custodial duration), not year-1-only — year-1 ramps at

~half steady-state over the 12-month migration (seed option: repayable

premium advance, UI trust-fund precedent). Three-leg ≈ ~$30–35/mo

pushes credit headroom. Belongings opt-in-vs-mandatory is the swing

variable.

  • Remedy path: administrative appeal → district court on what standard?
  • Referral-abuse controls (Brad 10/19 — "can they bribe the insurer into

criminal charges?"): private parties can't FILE charges, but they can

refer fraud to prosecutors — needs (a) penalty for knowingly-false

fraud referral, symmetric to manufactured-claim penalties (lives

inside I-7), (b) tenant notice + defense window before a referral

transmits, (c) referral evidence threshold. Corruption can't be

designed to zero — design for visible (registry), expensive

(penalties), appealable (independent review).

How the funding braid was decided — the history (teach-by)

*Kept so anyone picking this up learns why it looks this way, not just

what it says. Dated decisions, superseded choices, and the math fix.*

  • **RESOLVED 10/18, AMENDED 10/19 + 10/23** — funding = three-source

braid, not landlord-paid as first framed.

  • **Deposit** → bonded escrow at signing, held for the tenancy (UK

custodial model — landlord never holds it). Pooled float = permanent

funding leg; principal returns at move-out through adjudication;

clean years earn the renter a claims-free dividend on top of the

statutory 1%, declared from pool surplus when the books run ahead

(mutual model, not guaranteed payment). AMENDED 10/23 — supersedes

"refund after clean year."

  • **MN Renter's Credit (M1PR)** = the ongoing renter-side share,

structured as an advance payment — paid monthly to the pool,

reconciled on the tax return (federal APTC mechanism, not a debt

intercept). Enrollment rides the mandatory CRP e-file *data* rail;

over-cap renters pay the share directly (~$6/mo) — disclosed, not

hidden.

  • **Landlord matched share** = explicit third leg (Brad 10/19 merge —

"the other half"), PLUS the deposit float they no longer hold. Priced

community base + experience surcharge; clean-book years earn a

no-claim dividend, declared from surplus (10/23 — answers the

median-landlord "it's a tax" attack).

  • Split ratio = open pending actuarial. The 10/23 math correction:

usable float is the 3% spread, not 4% — the tenant's own 1% doesn't

fund the pool. Frozen MODEL_PROGRAM tenant-premium line still needs

reconcile in a future live version (SOURCES stay frozen).
