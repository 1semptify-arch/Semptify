Our Privacy Commitment

Semptify is privacy-respecting by design. We never store your documents on our servers. Your documents stay in your own cloud storage — Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive — under your control, under your account, under your terms.

What We Store

  • Account metadata — Your role, storage provider, and session state.
  • Timeline entries — Text descriptions of events (not the documents themselves).
  • Journal entries — Your written notes and records.
  • Verified facts — Sourced facts from our Context Engine (public law, not your data).

What We Don't Store

  • Your documents — Lease, photos, receipts, communications — all in your cloud storage.
  • Your files — PDFs, images, attachments — all in your cloud storage.
  • Your OAuth tokens — Stored in encrypted server memory only for active sessions.

Your Rights

  • Access — You can access your data at any time.
  • Deletion — You can delete your account and data at any time.
  • Portability — Your documents are already in your cloud storage — they're portable by design.
  • No advertising — We never sell your data. No advertising, ever.

Contact

For privacy questions or concerns: support@semptify.org
