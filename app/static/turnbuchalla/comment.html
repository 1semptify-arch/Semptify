Marginalia, made publicMark the draft

Every mark is numbered, dated, and attached to a clause. Signed marks enter the public record under your name; unsigned marks are still read and still count. Nothing here is hidden — that is the point.

Your mark is entered — it will appear in the public record once read into the ledger.
