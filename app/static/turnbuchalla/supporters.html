Signing the roll says one thing only: "I have read it, and I think Minnesota should carry it." No money, no party, no pledge — a name and a town, entered in the open, dated, and counted. Legislators read rolls like this one.

Your name is headed for the roll — it appears once entered into the ledger.

Prefer quiet? Sign by mail instead: brad@semptify.org — name and town is all it takes.

The roll is not yet open — the first names will appear here the moment signing opens. No names are published without being given.

A name on this roll is a hand raised — plainly, publicly, and once.
