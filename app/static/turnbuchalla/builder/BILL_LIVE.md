  10/23: base ≈ net float ~$3.25/mo + ~$6 renter-credit + ~$6 landlord
  match ≈ $15.33/mo at $1,300 rent. Net float = 3% of escrowed deposits
  (4% yield less the 1% statutory interest paid to the tenant — the
  earlier $4.33 figure counted the tenant's own 1%). Float is now an
  ONGOING leg (custodial duration), not year-1-only — year-1 ramps at
  ~half steady-state over the 12-month migration (seed option: repayable
  premium advance, UI trust-fund precedent). Three-leg ≈ ~$30–35/mo
  pushes credit headroom. Belongings opt-in-vs-mandatory is the swing
  variable.
- Remedy path: administrative appeal → district court on what standard?
- Referral-abuse controls (Brad 10/19 — "can they bribe the insurer into
  criminal charges?"): private parties can't FILE charges, but they can
  refer fraud to prosecutors — needs (a) penalty for knowingly-false fraud
  referral, symmetric to manufactured-claim penalties (probably lives
  inside clause 5), (b) tenant notice + defense window before a referral
  transmits, (c) referral evidence threshold. Corruption can't be designed
  to zero — design for visible (registry), expensive (penalties),
  appealable (independent review).

## RESOLVED 10/18, AMENDED 10/19 + 10/23 — funding source (three-source braid)
- **Deposit** → bonded escrow at signing, held **for the tenancy** (UK
  custodial model — landlord never holds it). Pooled float = permanent
  funding leg; deposit principal returns at move-out through adjudication;
  clean years earn the renter a **claims-free dividend** on top of the
  statutory 1% — declared from pool surplus when the books run ahead
  (mutual model, not a guaranteed payment). (AMENDED 10/23 — supersedes
  "refund after clean year".)
- **MN Renter's Credit (M1PR)** = the ongoing renter-side share, structured
  as an **advance payment** — paid monthly to the pool, reconciled on the
  tax return (federal APTC mechanism, not a debt intercept). Enrollment
  rides the mandatory CRP e-file *data* rail; over-cap renters pay the
  share directly (~$6/mo) — disclosed, not hidden.
- **Landlord matched share** = explicit third leg (per Brad 10/19 merge —
  "the other half"), PLUS the deposit float they no longer hold. Priced
  community base + experience surcharge; clean-book years earn a
  **no-claim dividend**, declared from surplus (added 10/23 — answers
  the median-landlord "it's a tax" attack).
- Split ratio = open drafting Q pending actuarial. CONCEPT_SHEET "Who pays"
  reconciled 10/19. Frozen MODEL_PROGRAM tenant-premium line still needs
  reconcile in the next live version (SOURCES stay frozen).
