## What this draft is — and is not

The 11/05 evaluation's call: drafting Title II as an immediate statewide
mandate invites a fiscal-note delay in committee. Drafted instead as a
**Minnesota Housing Finance Agency pilot on publicly-subsidized (LIHTC)
stock**, riding the compliance machinery that already exists for
tax-credit properties — owner certifications, income and rent reporting,
the qualified allocation plan — so the pilot *is* the fiscal note for
statewide rollout (BILL_V2 II-6). Statewide coverage happens only if
the legislature acts on the pilot's year-3 report and actuarials.

It does **not** contain: the custodial-escrow amendment to § 504B.178
(Title I — separate draft, `TITLE_I_STATUTORY_DRAFT_2026-11-05.md`),
belongings coverage (Title III — deferred pending actuarial), or the
governing board, public claims registry, manufactured-claim penalties,
and Act-wide sunset (Title IV — though this draft flags every place
Title II depends on them).

## Drafting conventions

- `[462A.46]` is the proposed home for the new section — it is the next
  free section number in chapter 462A (§ 462A.45, Lived-Experience
  Engagement Exemption, is the chapter's last). The Revisor assigns the
  real number; the bracket stays until then.
- Bracketed `[TBD]` marks = Revisor/counsel completion (time windows
  pending actuarial, designations pending the home-agency call, penalty
  cross-references living elsewhere in the Act).
- Subdivision numbering is provisional — the Revisor renumbers on
  insertion; the grouping is what matters.
- "Agency" = the Minnesota Housing Finance Agency (§ 462A.03).
- "This section" = § 462A.46 as proposed. "This Act" = the whole bill.

## THE TEXT

**Section 1.** Minnesota Statutes 2026, chapter 462A, is amended by
adding a section to read:

## [462A.46] MINNESOTA RENT ASSURANCE PILOT PROGRAM.

**Subd. 1. Definitions.**

(a) As used in this section, the following terms have the meanings
given.

(b) "Agency" means the Minnesota Housing Finance Agency.

(c) "Allocation agreement" means the extended low-income housing
commitment described in section 42(h)(6) of the Internal Revenue Code
of 1986, as amended, or a comparable recorded land use restriction
agreement administered by the agency.

(d) "Covered hardship" means a verified, involuntary loss of a tenant's
ability to pay rent arising from an event on the covered hardship list
established by the program administrator under subdivision 3,
paragraph (b), including involuntary job loss and medical crisis.

(e) "Pilot property" means a residential rental development that (1)
received an allocation of low-income housing tax credits under section
462A.222 or 462A.223[, or financing supported by the Minnesota housing
tax credit contribution account under section 462A.40 — counsel
question 6], and (2) is subject to the agency's compliance monitoring
under the allocation agreement.

(f) "Pool" means the Minnesota rent assurance pilot fund established
under subdivision 5.

(g) "Program administrator" means the agency or the administrator it
contracts under section 462A.05, subdivision 7.

(h) "Qualified allocation plan" or "QAP" means the qualified allocation
plan adopted by the agency as required by section 42(m)(1)(B) of the
Internal Revenue Code.

**Subd. 2. Pilot program established; scope.**

(a) The agency may establish and operate a Minnesota rent assurance
pilot program to pay covered rent to owners of pilot properties during
verified tenant covered hardship. The pilot is a demonstration program
within the meaning of section 462A.05, subdivision 41, and this
section is the specific authorization for it.

(b) The agency may provide for participation in the pilot by:

(1) making participation a selection criterion or a threshold condition
in the qualified allocation plan for developments receiving a new
allocation of low-income housing tax credits; and

(2) accepting the written participation agreement of an owner of a
pilot property not otherwise required to participate.

(c) Except for the enrollment and claims information required by this
section, the agency shall administer the pilot using the existing
allocation agreement compliance monitoring for a pilot property and
shall impose no new licensing requirement on an owner by reason of
participation. [This is the "QAP rails" clause — the pilot rides the
reporting infrastructure already in place for tax-credit stock rather
than building a parallel registry.]

(d) This section does not create a statewide mandate. No person is
required to participate except as provided in paragraph (b).

**Subd. 3. Covered rent benefit.**

(a) Upon approval of a claim under subdivision 6, the pool shall pay
to the owner the covered rent for a pilot-property unit for the period
of the tenant's verified covered hardship, not to exceed [TBD — 2 to
6 months per tenancy, needs actuarial] in any [TBD-month] period, and
subject to the per-lease coverage cap established by the program
administrator.

(b) The program administrator shall publish a covered hardship list
and verification standards modeled on unemployment insurance
determination practice. [UI-style verification precedent per
BILL_V2 II-1; the trigger list is an open drafting question per
BILL_LIVE — the list itself is the actuarial swing variable.]

(c) A payment under this section supplements, and does not duplicate,
assistance under the family homeless prevention and assistance
program, section 462A.204, or other emergency rental assistance.
Payment for the same rent obligation for the same month may be made
under only one program; receipt or application under another program
does not otherwise disqualify a tenant or owner under this section.
[Carries the BILL_LIVE open question on FHPAP interaction into a
coordination clause — no double recovery, no disqualification.]

**Subd. 4. No subrogation; forward-rent plan only.**

(a) Payment of a claim under this section extinguishes any right of
the agency, the program administrator, the owner, or any other person
to recover the amount paid from the tenant, including by subrogation,
assignment, indemnity, debt collection, or any other right of
recovery. [Locked clause 1 — applies across all titles.]

(b) A repayment plan applies solely to rent accruing after the coverage
period and is a condition of continued coverage, not of the claim.
[REMEDIATION H5 one-liner, made statutory.]

(c) A contract term providing for recovery against a tenant of an
amount paid or payable under this section is void and unenforceable.

(d) A person that seeks recovery from a tenant of an amount paid or
payable under this section is subject to the penalties of section
[TBD — manufactured-claim penalties, Title IV / locked clause 5].

**Subd. 5. Pool; funding.**

(a) The Minnesota rent assurance pilot fund is established in the
state treasury as a separate account administered by the agency
[within the housing development fund, section 462A.20 — TBD confirm
fund placement]. The pool consists of the funding sources in this
subdivision and any amounts appropriated, gifted, or granted to it
under section 462A.05, subdivision 6. Earnings on pool balances are
credited to the pool.

(b) *Owner matched share.* Each participating owner shall pay a
matched share rated on a community base plus an experience-rated
surcharge within a bounded band established by the program
administrator. In a year in which the pool's books run ahead, the
program administrator may declare a no-claim dividend to an owner
whose pilot units generated no paid claim, paid from pool surplus. A
dividend is declared, not guaranteed. [Locked 10/23 — community base +
bounded experience surcharge; mutual-model dividend per SFM precedent.]

(c) *Renter-side share.* A renter-side share is carried for a covered
tenancy by a monthly advance against the renter's credit under section
290.0693, paid to the pool and reconciled on the renter's tax return
for the taxable year. Enrollment rides the certificate-of-rent-paid
data furnished to the commissioner of revenue under section 290.0693,
subdivision 4. A renter whose credit does not cover the share pays the
remainder directly [~$6/month at the 10/23 restated split], and the
charge must be disclosed at enrollment. [Federal APTC / Form 8962
reconciliation model — locked 10/23; H2/H3/H8.]

(d) *Deposit float share.* [TBD — contingent. The third braid leg is
net float on custodial-escrowed deposits under the Title I amendment
to section 504B.178. If Title I is not enacted, either (i) the pilot
runs on the two legs in paragraphs (b) and (c) repriced by the
actuary, or (ii) the pilot escrows pilot-property deposits under this
section — which also generates Title I actuarial data. NEEDS BRAD +
counsel: drafting escrow inside ch. 462A for pilot properties only
duplicates custody machinery the Act writes statewide in ch. 504B;
running two legs breaks the locked three-source braid for the pilot's
duration. Default shown here: paragraph (d) is dormant until Title I
or pilot escrow authority is added.]

(e) *Year-one seed.* [TBD — pick one and state it: (1) a repayable
advance to the pool from the housing development fund under section
462A.21, modeled on the unemployment insurance trust fund advance
mechanism, repaid from premiums; or (2) coverage phasing in only as
pool reserves cross a threshold set by the actuary. Drafted default is
(1) the repayable advance — a pilot cannot wait a year for reserves
to accumulate the way a statewide pool can. REMEDIATION H7 / Tier 3.]

(f) Premium and share amounts are established by the program
administrator on actuarial advice [TBD — rate cap vs. administrator-set
with oversight is an open question in BILL_LIVE].

**Subd. 6. Claims; adjudication.**

(a) An owner seeking payment of covered rent, or a tenant contesting
a coverage or hardship determination, shall file a claim with the
adjudication body designated under section [TBD — independent
adjudication body, Title IV / locked clause 3; pilot administration
may be contracted to a licensed insurer under section 462A.05,
subdivision 7, per the SFM precedent — NEEDS BRAD confirm].

(b) The adjudication body shall provide the parties notice, an
opportunity to be heard, and a written decision. The burden of proving
a covered hardship and the covered rent amount is on the claimant by
a fair preponderance of the evidence. [Tenant counter-claim lane —
locked clause 3; burden allocation mirrors Title I subd. 3a(e).]

(c) An appeal from an adjudication decision may be brought in the
district court in the county where the pilot property is located or,
at the option of the tenant, in the county of the owner's residence.
The standard of review is [TBD — same open question as Title I
counsel question 3].

**Subd. 7. Data; actuarial reporting.**

(a) The program administrator shall collect enrollment, claims,
payment, and hardship-verification data through the compliance
monitoring rail and claim files created under this section. Data on
individual tenants and owners collected under this section are
[private / nonpublic — TBD; needs a Minnesota Government Data
Practices Act, chapter 13, classification clause]. Aggregate
claims data on pilot properties may be published [cross-reference:
public claims registry, Title IV / locked clause 6 — the pilot's
published data is the registry's first cohort].

(b) The program administrator shall obtain an annual actuarial
valuation of the pool, including incurred and projected claims,
premium and share adequacy, and administrative cost. Data collected
and valuations produced under this section constitute the fiscal
analysis for statewide rollout of the rent assurance program.
[BILL_V2 II-6 — pilot data IS the fiscal note.]

**Subd. 8. Year-three report; statewide rollout contingent.**

(a) No later than [TBD — within 90 days after the end of the pilot's
third full coverage year], the agency shall report to the chairs and
ranking minority members of the legislative committees with
jurisdiction over housing finance. The report must include: pilot
enrollment; claims experience and loss ratios; per-unit delivered
cost; coverage and hardship-term adequacy; administrative cost; the
experience-surcharge and dividend results; and a recommendation
whether the program should be enacted statewide, with proposed
amendatory language. [Locked clause 7 — sunset + year-3 report,
adapted to the pilot: the report is the decision point, not a
sunset alone.]

(b) Statewide operation of a rent assurance program may be enacted
only by subsequent legislation. Nothing in this section authorizes
statewide expansion by rule or agency action.

**Subd. 9. Expiration.**

Authority to enroll new properties under this section expires [TBD —
align with the Title IV sunset proposal: five years after the first
coverage year]. Claims pending on that date are completed under this
section, and unencumbered pool balances [revert to the housing
development fund / TBD].

## THE QAP RAILS — what already exists vs. what this draft adds

The pilot's design bet is that tax-credit stock already reports
everything a guarantee pool needs to underwrite and verify. The table
maps each pilot function to the rail it rides.

| Pilot function | Existing rail it rides | What the draft adds |
|---|---|---|
| Identifying eligible stock | Agency administers federal credit allocations under §§ 462A.222–.223 (designated housing credit agency) | "Pilot property" definition; opt-in agreement for existing stock |
| Enrolling new properties | QAP selection/threshold criteria under 26 U.S.C. § 42(m)(1)(B) | Participation as a QAP criterion or condition — counsel question 2 |
| Owner compliance reporting | Annual owner certifications, income and rent data under the allocation agreement | Claims and enrollment data only; no new licensing (subd. 2(c)) |
| Renter-side enrollment | Certificate of rent paid furnished to DOR under § 290.0693 subd. 4 | Monthly credit-advance election + reconciliation |
| Hardship verification | [none — new function] | UI-model verification standards by rule (subd. 3(b)) |
| Paying the benefit | Agency fund + contract machinery (§§ 462A.20, 462A.05 subd. 7) | The pool account and claim payment |
| Producing the fiscal note | Agency reporting practice | Annual actuarial valuation + year-3 report (subds. 7–8) |

## DEPENDENCIES THIS DRAFT DOES NOT CREATE

Like Title I, this draft points at entities the Act creates elsewhere:

1. **Independent adjudication body** — locked clause 3; Title IV
   designation slot [TBD]. The pilot cannot run claims through an
   owner-sympathetic or administrator-internal process.
2. **Manufactured-claim penalties + referral-abuse controls** — locked
   clause 5 + Brad 10/19 controls (symmetric false-referral penalty,
   tenant notice and defense window). Subd. 4(d) cross-references them.
3. **Public claims registry** — locked clause 6; pilot aggregate data
   becomes its first cohort. FCRA-modeled accuracy/dispute rules live
   in Title IV.
4. **Governing board with tenant/consumer seats** — locked clause 4.
   A pilot run solely inside the agency risks exactly the capture the
   board exists to prevent — flag for counsel whether pilot governance
   needs an interim advisory seat structure. [Found while drafting.]
5. **Home agency / program administrator confirmation** — BILL_V2
   IV-2 proposes MHFA as fund home; the pilot is already drafted at
   MHFA, which makes the pilot the natural proof-of-home. NEEDS BRAD
   confirm remains.

**Braid note — a real divergence to surface, not hide:** locked clause
2 (mandatory participation) and II-3's three-source braid are stated
for the statewide program. On LIHTC stock the "mandatory" element
converts to a QAP condition plus opt-in, and the float leg cannot run
until Title I or a pilot-escrow clause exists (subd. 5(d)). The
10/23 restated split (~$3.25 float + ~$6 renter-credit + ~$6 owner
match ≈ $15.33/mo at $1,300 rent) therefore does not price the pilot
as drafted — the pilot's own actuarial sets its split. That is the
point of the pilot: the numbers stop being modeled.

## QUESTIONS FOR COUNSEL (the 504B author)

1. **Existing authority.** Section 462A.05, subdivision 41, already
   authorizes demonstration grants to owners/managers of multifamily
   rental property the agency holds a mortgage on, and subdivision 7
   gives general contract power. Does the agency already have authority
   to run a voluntary pilot without this section — and if so, should
   this section instead be drafted as *direction plus the braid's
   mandatory features* (premium mechanics, no-subrogation, advance
   credit), while a voluntary demo starts immediately under existing
   power? (TASKS.md already notes "MHFA pilot anytime, needs no bill."
   This draft may be the statewide-path vehicle, not the pilot's
   ignition key — the two lanes need one sentence of reconciliation.)
2. **QAP conditioning.** Federal § 42(m) requires the QAP to set
   selection criteria serving stated preferences. Is rent-assurance
   participation a permissible criterion or threshold condition for
   new allocations — and can it touch existing allocations' compliance
   terms without reopening recorded extended-use agreements?
3. **Credit advance mechanics.** Who runs the monthly renter-side
   advance — the commissioner of revenue against section 290.0693
   (credit + CRP data rail, subd. 4), or the agency against
   administrator-verified enrollment? The APTC model suggests DOR
   data + agency disbursement; the drafting home for the advance
   machinery is the open point (ch. 290 amendment vs. ch. 462A
   direction).
4. **No-subrogation on subsidized stock.** Does conditioning credit
   allocation or participation on no-recourse terms create any
   conflict with section 42 compliance obligations or recorded LURAs
   — and does subd. 4(a) need a federal-subsidy carve-in?
5. **Data practices.** Tenant hardship files sit on the seam between
   chapter 13 classifications and the FCRA-modeled registry rules the
   Act builds in Title IV. What classification clause does subd. 7(a)
   need so the pilot's data is usable for the year-3 report without
   exposing tenant records?
6. **Found while drafting — which credit stock counts.** Section
   462A.40 is the *contribution account* for the Minnesota housing
   tax credit, not the allocation authority — the pilot-property
   definition keys on §§ 462A.222–.223 federal-credit stock and
   brackets 462A.40. Confirm whether the pilot should reach
   state-credit-supported properties and through what authority.
7. **Found while drafting — CRP rail moved.** The certificate-of-rent-
   paid obligation for ordinary landlords now sits in § 290.0693
   subd. 4 (the renter's credit section, new 2023); § 290A.19 covers
   manufactured-home park owners only. Live docs still say "CRP
   e-file data rail" — the rail is real, but its statutory home
   moved. This draft cites the current one; flag for a consistency
   pass on BILL_LIVE when the credit-advance mechanics settle.
