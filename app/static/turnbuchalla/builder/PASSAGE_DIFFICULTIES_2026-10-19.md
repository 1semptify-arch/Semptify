# PASSAGE DIFFICULTIES — what stands between this and law

*2026-10-19. Ranked by lethality — Tier 1 kills it, Tier 2 wounds it,
Tier 3 is friction. Each carries the counter already in the design.
Sources: CHAMPION_MAP (10/14), DECISIONS, BILL_LIVE, research memo Parts
B/D/E.*

## Tier 1 — kills it dead

1. **No carrier = no bill.** The entire project currently hinges on a
   member agreeing to author. Howard and Igo are both needed; Port's
   announced non-re-election means Senate continuity runs through a
   second member (Mohamed). Until a staff briefing converts, there is no
   bill — only a model. *Counter already in design:* Birnberg reads first
   (his fixes become the draft, his name carries), Jetty timing is the
   hook, the two-lane pitch is written per office.

2. **The fiscal note.** If actuaries price claims above the model,
   "self-funding" dies in committee and it becomes "another program" —
   the exact attack the concept sheet pre-answers. *Counter:* FHF/
   Minneapolis Fed validation email goes BEFORE the member asks (queued);
   belongings leg deferred to post-actuarial (Brad, 10/19); the MHFA
   pilot generates real data even if the bill stalls.

3. **MMHA (landlord lobby).** Mandatory participation + deposits leaving
   landlord hands = two threats to one lobby, and they testify on every
   housing bill. *Counter:* experience rating splits honest landlords
   from serial filers — the industry's clean majority gets a discount;
   net-cost math (premium < eviction spend); champion map says brief them
   late, not first.

## Tier 2 — wounds it

4. **Mechanism legitimacy.** M1PR assignment to a pool needs a DOR
   mechanism — committee counsel's first question is "can you even route
   a tax credit there?" *Counter:* the bridge-options memo (assignment /
   opt-in / escrow timing) is queued work; CRP e-file rail shows the
   reporting plumbing already exists.

5. **"The state takes your deposit" attack.** Legally weak — deposits
   remain tenant property in bonded escrow — but rhetorically sticky.
   *Counter:* the honest framing is the deposit is a one-year collateral
   loan that refunds after a clean year; the real transfer is the float
   landlords currently keep.

6. **Scope creep.** Rent + deposits + belongings + registry + new
   adjudication = a complex bill, and committees kill complexity.
   *Counter:* belongings deferred; the pilot demonstrates the core
   without the full build.

## Tier 3 — friction, not fatal

7. **Insurtech opposition** — Rhino/Jetty/Obligo fight for their
   product's life; "let the market handle it" testimony. *Counter:* their
   own litigation record is the answer (Jetty SDNY, filed Oct 6).

8. **Court-filing ecosystem** — collection attorneys and process servers
   quietly oppose; the judge-visible court-cost savings is the counter.

9. **Crowded docket** — 2027 session housing bills compete; pre-session
   deadlines ~Nov–Dec 2026. Champion strength is the only mitigation.

10. **Non-filer gap** — highest-risk renters least likely to file M1 /
    claim the credit. *Counter:* the CRP rail auto-flags eligibility —
    the landlord files the claim document, not the tenant.

11. **Sponsorship credibility** — Semptify is pre-501(c), and the founder
    is a pro se litigant against a landlord. *Counter:* the locked rule —
    case = exhibit only if asked; Birnberg's read is the credibility
    bridge; mechanism sells, grievance doesn't.

## The structural truth

A citizen bill lives or dies on whether an **institution adopts it** —
committee staff, an agency, or a validation shop. Phase 2 exists to
convert "Brad's model" into "the model FHF checked and Birnberg read."
Every difficulty above weakens the moment that conversion happens — and
strengthens every week it doesn't.

## What survives the difficulties

- The Jetty window (fresh, national, on-point)
- The bipartisan two-lane frame (consumer protection / court-cost
  savings — each side hears their own reason)
- The pilot fallback (MHFA needs no bill — the project can't fully fail)
- A funding story internally consistent as of 10/19
