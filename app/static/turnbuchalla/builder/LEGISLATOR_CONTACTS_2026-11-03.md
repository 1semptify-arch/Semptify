## HOW THIS BILL MOVES (who has clout, in order)

1. **A member must carry it.** Bill needs a chief author in each chamber. House:
   Housing Finance & Policy co-chairs decide what gets heard — **Howard (DFL) and
   Igo (R)** — the House is tied 67-67 under a power-sharing deal, so BOTH co-chairs
   must agree. Senate: **Port** chairs Housing until January; **Mohamed** is continuity.
2. **Committee staff are the real door.** Staff briefings convert before member asks —
   committee administrators control the hearing calendar.
3. **The committee gauntlet:** Housing → Taxes (M1PR credit rail) → Commerce
   (insurance/deposit-alternative products) → Judiciary/Civil Law (§504B amendments)
   → Ways & Means/Finance (fiscal note) → Rules → floor.
4. **The Governor signs.** Agency route in parallel: **MHFA can pilot without a bill**
   (Baumtrog/Asst. Commissioner is the pilot-track door; Commissioner Ho until ~Jan 4).
   **Commerce** regulates the insurance/deposit-alternative products the bill converts.
   **Revenue** owns the M1PR/CRP machinery the renter share rides on.
5. **Outside clout:** Minnesota Housing Partnership, HOME Line, Housing Justice Center
   testify in every housing hearing. **MMHA (landlord lobby) is the opposition — brief
   them late, not first** (champion-map rule).
