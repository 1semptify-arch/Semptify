How a claim is checkedThe process behind the shelf

Every figure travels the same road before it may be quoted:

1

Source it. The claim is written next to its source — an official publication, a statute, or a filed document. No source, no sentence.

2

Check it twice. Two independent confirmations on different dates earn the ✓✓ mark. A single confirmation earns ✓ and stays labeled.

3

Or flag it. Internal estimates and figures awaiting published data are marked estimate with the derivation shown — never presented as settled fact.

4

And stay correctable. Any reader may flag an error at the Inkwell; a correction entered into the record is credited to the person who caught it.

Find a number that won't hold? Mark it at the Inkwell — a caught error here is a win, not an embarrassment.

A claim worth making is a claim worth sourcing.
