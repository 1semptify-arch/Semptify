# Context Explanation Needs Checklist

This is the editorial checklist for `context_explanation_entries`.

For every subject/pillar combination below, a human author must write four plain-text variants:
- **mechanics** — 1st exposure
- **trust** — 2nd exposure
- **reinforcement** — 3rd exposure
- **minimal** — 4th+ exposure

Entries are keyed by `subject` + `jurisdiction` + `pillar` + `review_status`. The default jurisdiction is `MN`.

Priority `HIGH` rows are the 10-entry starter set. Fill those first.

---

## Discrimination (`discrimination`)

### GOVERN — priority HIGH

- **Context of use:** The tenant is in a high-stakes or legally risky situation for discrimination.
- **UPL risk tier:** HIGH
- **Review status:** BETA

- **mechanics** — what the risk is and why the tenant should get help. Target 40-80 words.
- **trust** — why this situation needs careful handling and possibly an advocate. Target 25-50 words.
- **reinforcement** — a quick reminder of the risk and the need for help. Target 15-35 words.
- **minimal** — a one-line risk/status cue. Target 3-10 words.
