What Semptify does

Semptify helps you organize your tenancy records. Your documents stay in your own cloud storage — not on our servers. That means you keep control, and we never see the contents of your files.

There are no accounts to create, no passwords to forget, and no tracking. Just your records, in order, when you need them.

Before you start

  • You choose your cloud storage
  • Semptify only reads and writes folders you approve
  • Everything is encrypted in transit by your provider
  • You can disconnect at any time
