❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I have to respond to an eviction notice in Minnesota? ▼
Typically 7 days to file a written answer with the court after being served. If you don't respond, a default judgment may be issued. Do not ignore a summons. Call Legal Aid (1-888-543-5110) or HOME Line (612-728-5767) immediately.
Can I be evicted in winter in Minnesota? ▼
Yes. No general winter moratorium exists. Proceedings continue year-round. Sheriffs have discretion over physical removal in dangerous cold, but this is not a legal right. Always attend your hearing regardless of season.
What if my landlord won't make repairs? ▼
MN Stat. 504B.161 requires landlords to maintain habitable conditions. Remedies: Rent Escrow (pay court instead of landlord), Repair and Deduct (under conditions), Habitability Defense in eviction. Document everything first — use your Semptify journal.
Can I sue my landlord back? ▼
Yes. A counterclaim lets you sue in the same eviction case. Common: habitability violations, illegal lockout, retaliation, security deposit violations, discrimination. Can reduce/eliminate what you owe or award damages.
Do I need a lawyer? ▼
You can represent yourself ("pro se"). Semptify helps you organize. But a lawyer improves outcomes. Free legal help: Legal Aid 1-888-543-5110, HOME Line 612-728-5767, lawhelpmn.org.
What should I bring to court? ▼
Written answer/response, signed lease, all rent receipts, written repair requests, dated photos, all landlord communications, your Semptify journal timeline, government ID. Organize chronologically — judges need to see what happened and when.
