The Cause Behind Semptify

We built this because people facing housing problems deserve the same tools that attorneys have — and they deserve them for free, without someone profiting from their crisis.

Why This Exists

Every year, hundreds of thousands of people face eviction, uninhabitable conditions, illegal rent increases, and landlord retaliation. The legal system can help them — but only if they can organize their evidence, meet their deadlines, communicate clearly, and show up with the right documents.

Most people in a housing crisis do not have a lawyer. They do not have money for one. They are often working multiple jobs, managing children, dealing with health problems, or simply overwhelmed by a system that was not designed with them in mind.

Semptify exists to close that gap. Not by replacing attorneys — we never claim to do that — but by giving people the same organizational tools, the same record-keeping discipline, and the same document-ready posture that a well-funded legal team would provide.

When a tenant has organized evidence, a complete timeline, and records of every communication, their landlord has far fewer excuses. That is what we build for.

Who We Serve

🏠

Tenants

People renting a home who need to document conditions, track rent payments, record communications, and build a clear record of their tenancy. This is our primary user and our first priority in every decision.

🤝

Advocates

Housing counselors, tenant unions, and community organizers who help multiple tenants at once. Semptify helps them manage cases, deliver documents securely, and stay organized without spreadsheets.

⚖️

Legal Aid & Attorneys

Legal professionals working in housing law who need organized case files, evidence trails, and court-ready documents. We build to MN Supreme Court exhibit standards (MNDES ADM09-8010).

🔬

Researchers & Policy

Academics and policy professionals studying housing patterns. We provide aggregate, anonymized insights — never individual data — to support better housing policy.

Exactly What Data We Use — And What We Do Not

We believe you should know precisely what we collect and why. Here is the complete picture.

We NEVER collect or keep

  • 🚫 Your document contents
  • 🚫 Which pages you visit or for how long
  • 🚫 Which buttons or features you use
  • 🚫 Your IP address (beyond routing)
  • 🚫 Your device type, browser, or OS
  • 🚫 Your physical location
  • 🚫 Your search terms inside the app
  • 🚫 How many times you tap anything
  • 🚫 Any behavioral or usage analytics
  • 🚫 Third-party tracking cookies
  • 🚫 Advertising identifiers

We ONLY keep (engineering use only)

  • ✅ Server error logs (anonymised, auto-deleted)
  • ✅ API response times (no user identifiers)
  • ✅ Crash reports (stack traces, no user data)
  • ✅ Rate limit counters (IP-based, ephemeral)
  • ✅ Session tokens (signed, stored in your browser only)
  • ✅ Onboarding gate state (which steps are complete)

Technical data is used only to keep the platform running correctly. It is never used to understand user behaviour, target content, or report to any third party.

How Semptify Works

When you connect to Semptify, you connect your own cloud storage account — Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive. Your documents go directly into your storage. Semptify is the organisation layer: it helps you tag documents, build a timeline, track communications, and generate court-ready exhibits.

When you leave Semptify — if you ever do — your documents are still in your account, in your folders, exactly where you put them. We do not have copies. We cannot hold your files hostage. There is no lock-in.

How We Keep the Lights On

Semptify runs entirely on donations and grants. That is the whole model. No investors. No ad revenue. No data sales. No subscription tiers. No "freemium" plan waiting to lock you in.

If you find Semptify useful and want to support it, a donation directly funds server costs, development time, and the ongoing work of building tools for people who need them most. Every dollar goes to the mission — none of it to shareholders, none of it to growth teams, none of it to marketing.

If you cannot donate, that is completely fine. Using Semptify, sharing it with someone who needs it, and giving us feedback are all valuable contributions. The tool exists to be used, not to be monetised.

What We Believe

⚖️

The Law Is the Law

When the law protects a tenant, we say so clearly. We do not soften legal facts to avoid controversy. We do not hedge to create false balance.

📋

Evidence Integrity First

Every feature is evaluated on whether it improves rights protection, evidence preservation, or user control. Features that optimise for engagement or growth are not our priority.

🔒

Privacy Is Not a Feature

Privacy is not a selling point we can remove if business demands change. It is a structural commitment built into how the platform works.

🧭

Clarity Under Stress

Our users are often scared, overwhelmed, and short on time. Clear, plain-language guidance beats impressive jargon every time.

🌐

Public Infrastructure

Housing justice tools should be public goods, not proprietary products. Open source by default. Anyone can inspect, fork, or build on what we make.

🤲

Conviction, Not Commerce

We are not building toward an acquisition or an IPO. We are building something that should exist and currently does not exist well enough.

Semptify is a labor of conviction. This platform was built by people who believe that technology should serve those who need it most — not those who can pay the most. We have no investors, no growth team, and no engagement metrics to hit. We measure success by whether someone was able to protect their housing because of what we built. We are sustained by grants, donations, and the effort of people who believe housing justice matters. If that resonates with you, we would be grateful for your support — and even more grateful if you use the tool and share it with someone who needs it.
