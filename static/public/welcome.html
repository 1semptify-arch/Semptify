What is going wrong?

Semptify helps you record what is happening, find plain-language facts about your rights, and take the next right step. No legal jargon. No pressure.

If you are dealing with a repair problem, a notice from your landlord, a missing security deposit, or any other housing issue, the best first step is to write it down.

What you can do here

Record

Save documents, photos, dates, and communications in one timeline.

Know

Read plain-language guides to tenant rights in your state.

Act

Find template letters, deadlines, and next steps.

Get help

If you need legal advice, connect with a local legal aid society.

We're not lawyers

Semptify is an organizational and educational tool, not a law firm. We cannot give legal advice or tell you what to do in your specific situation.

For legal advice about your specific situation, contact a licensed attorney or your local legal aid society.
