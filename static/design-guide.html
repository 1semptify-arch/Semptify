Callouts / Infoboxes

Used in Law Library, rights explanations, and AI Copilot responses. Not interchangeable with notices — callouts are educational, notices are transactional.

All 4 Callout Variants

💡 Tip — Document everything

Every communication with your landlord should be in writing. Send emails instead of phone calls, and follow up verbal conversations with a written summary.

⚖ Law — Implied Warranty of Habitability

Under most state laws, landlords are legally required to maintain rental units in a habitable condition. This includes functional heating, plumbing, and structural safety. (See: Implied Warranty of Habitability)

⚠ Caution — Do not withhold rent without counsel

Rent withholding is a legal remedy in some states, but only under specific conditions. Doing it incorrectly can give your landlord grounds for eviction. Consult a local tenant attorney first.

NOT LEGAL ADVICE

This information is for educational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Your situation may differ. Consult a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction for guidance specific to your case.

Forbidden UI Text

These words must NEVER appear in any UI text, button labels, headings, or help text: free · accounts · log in · sign up · subscription · upgrade · premium · paid plan · trial · pricing

Color Usage Rules

Color Intent Matrix
Color Token Use for Never use for
Primary Navy --color-primary Headings, active states, primary actions, link text Decorative fills
Accent Amber --color-accent Primary CTA buttons, important action affordances General highlights, decorative elements
Alarm Orange --color-alarm Real legal deadlines: eviction dates, court dates, response windows Everything else. Seriously.
Calm Blue-Gray --color-calm Secondary info, non-urgent callouts, draft states Primary actions
Error Red --color-error Form errors, destructive actions, overdue statuses Non-error emphasis

Accessibility Checklist

  • All interactive elements are keyboard-accessible (Tab + Enter/Space)
  • Focus ring visible: outline: 2px solid var(--color-primary); outline-offset: 2px;
  • Minimum contrast: 4.5:1 for body text (WCAG AA)
  • Icon-only buttons require aria-label
  • Tab panels use role="tablist", role="tab", role="tabpanel"
  • Flash bars and deadline notices use role="alert" or role="status"
  • Reduced motion: skeletons and transitions respect prefers-reduced-motion
  • Color is never the only indicator of meaning (always paired with text/icon)
